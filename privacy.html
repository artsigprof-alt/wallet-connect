Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Tank Game respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when using the game and related services.

Information We Collect

The game may collect limited information required for gameplay and wallet functionality, including:

  • Public Solana wallet addresses
  • Basic game progress and settings

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide game features, save progress, connect wallets, improve stability, and maintain security.

Wallet Information

Tank Game never requests or stores private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords. Only publicly available wallet addresses may be used for connection and gameplay features.

Data Storage

Some information may be stored locally on your device to preserve settings and game progress.

Third-Party Services

The game may interact with third-party services such as the Solana blockchain, wallet providers, social platforms, or application stores. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Tank Game is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws in their region.
